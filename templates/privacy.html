Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Overview

This privacy policy explains how noisefigurecalculator.com ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how your data is handled.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or process any personally identifiable information (PII) directly. Specifically:

  • All calculator inputs (noise power readings, ENR values) are processed entirely in your browser and on our server in real time. We do not store or log any measurement data you enter.
  • We do not require you to create an account or provide any personal information to use this site.
  • We do not use cookies of our own.

Google AdSense and Third-Party Advertising

This site uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google AdSense may use cookies and web beacons to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

For more information on how Google uses data when you use our site, visit Google's Privacy & Terms.

Analytics and Server Logs

Our hosting provider (Render) may collect standard server log information including IP addresses, browser type, referring pages, and access timestamps. This data is used solely for the purpose of operating and maintaining the service and is not shared with third parties.

European Users (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you may see a consent banner when visiting our site. This is managed through Google's Consent Management Platform. You have the right to accept or decline personalized advertising. Non-personalized ads may still be shown if you decline.

Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the site after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can contact us at contact@noisefigurecalculator.com.
